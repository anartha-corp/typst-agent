# Brand boundary

Typst Agent is an unofficial downstream of Typst. Typst and related marks are
owned by their respective owners. This project does not claim affiliation,
endorsement, or sponsorship by Typst GmbH.

The repository and release artifacts use the `typst-agent` name and their own
visual identity. Commercial distribution that uses the Typst name is blocked
until written permission is recorded by a maintainer. Community source releases
must preserve upstream notices and follow the applicable trademark guidance.
